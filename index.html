Meet Searching for Home Read my letter
Dear friend,

I began my writing career in 1972, when John Boy Walton scribbled in his Big Chief writing tablet. I was about eleven, and I’ve been filling notebooks ever since.

Along the way I raised five children, spent 24 years homeschooling, and wrote children’s books about hope and faith that, to my delight, have won a few awards. I’ve been blogging for over ten years. These days the nest is empty, there are three grandchildren to spoil, and there is finally a little time to chase the places I love.

I’m a hardcore Francophile and Italophile. Give me a sunny café table, something warm from the bakery, and a story to tell. So I packed up our chaos-filled American life, took part of my crew to Europe, and went searching for that elusive “home” feeling. That journey became my new book.

I’m so glad you’re here. Pull up a chair and stay a while.

xo, Debbie

Searching for Home

The European Journey of a Frazzled Mom

“A busy mom of five packed up our chaos-filled American life and took part of my crew to Europe in search of that elusive ‘home’ feeling.”

This is the story of that trip, through France, Italy and Austria. Each chapter blends personal stories with destination details, travel tips, and fascinating historical insights, so you can laugh along with the chaos and plan your own adventure at the same time.

Underneath it all is the question I carried the whole way:

What does “home” really mean?
