The New Memoir

Searching for Home

“A busy mom of five packed up our chaos-filled American life and took part of my crew to Europe in search of that elusive ‘home’ feeling.” Debbie Spence

Searching for Home – The European Journey of a Frazzled Mom is a travel memoir about one family’s journey through France, Italy and Austria, and the meaning of home they found along the way.

Each chapter blends personal stories with destination details, travel tips, and fascinating historical insights. It is a book to read with a café crème in hand, or tuck into your carry-on for the trip you have been dreaming about.

Along the way there are sunlit squares, long lunches and plenty of family chaos, and every road leads back to one question.

la question

What does “home” really mean?

About Debbie

A writer since a Big Chief tablet

“I began my writing career in 1972 when John Boy Walton scribbled in his Big Chief writing tablet.”

I was about eleven years old, and I have been writing ever since. Today I’m a multiple award-winning author of children’s books built on hope and faith, and I write for women who are trying to find a little more joy in their everyday lives. I have been blogging for over ten years.

I’m also a mom of five, a 24-year homeschooling veteran, an empty nester and a grandmother. Above all I’m a dedicated Francophile and Italophile. My love of France and Italy is what inspired Searching for Home.
